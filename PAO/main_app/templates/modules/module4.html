Healthcare in Bahrain

💡 Remember: Your employer must pay for your health care.

When You're Sick

If you're sick, your employer must consider and respect your health needs. Here's what to do:

1. Tell Your Employer

Inform your employer immediately if you feel unwell

2. Ask for Rest

Request more time to rest and recover

3. See a Doctor

Call a doctor at the health centre or visit a hospital

⚠️ Don't depend only on over-the-counter medicine, like Panadol. Always see a doctor for proper medical care.

Women's Health

Menstrual Health

Sanitary products like pads, pantyliners, and tampons can be found in most supermarkets.

💡 Tip: If you need these products, you can purchase them at local supermarkets or ask your employer for assistance.

Pregnancy

⚠️ IMPORTANT INFORMATION:

If you are unmarried and get pregnant while working in Bahrain, you may risk losing your job and may not be able to continue working in the country. You won't get any notice from your employer.

Protection from Sexual Abuse

If you face sexual abuse, or any unwanted sexual encounter, or become pregnant by forced sexual encounter:

✓ You have the right to seek help

✓ You should report it immediately

📞 Call for Help:

  • 995 - LMRA Helpline
  • 999 - Emergency Services

💜 Remember: Sexual assault is NEVER your fault.

Shamsaha Support

You can also get help from an organisation called Shamsaha:

  • English Hotline: +973 38447588 (24/7)
  • Arabic Hotline: +973 66710901 (24/7)
  • Website: shamsaha.org

💡 Download the Shamsaha app on your mobile phone for easy access to support services.

Occupational Health and Safety in the Workplace

Working with Bleaches and Cleaning Products

🧤 Wear Rubber Gloves

Always wear rubber gloves which should be replaced regularly (your employer must provide these)

📖 Read Instructions

Carefully read the instructions on cleaning products before using them

⚠️ Watch for Allergic Reactions

If you have an allergic reaction, tell your employer immediately

⚠️ Never mix different cleaning products together, as this can create dangerous chemical reactions.

Dealing with Medical Issues

Step 1: Tell Your Employer

If you feel sick, you must tell your employer right away

Step 2: Call a Doctor

Call a doctor at the health centre for professional medical advice

Step 3: Seek Proper Treatment

Don't depend only on over-the-counter medicine like Panadol - get proper medical care

Driving Safety

If you need to drive as part of your work, follow these important safety rules:

🚗 Drive Carefully

Drive carefully at all times and stay alert

🔒 Always Fasten Your Seatbelt

Wear your seatbelt every time you're in a vehicle

⚡ Pay Attention to Speed Limits

Observe speed limit signs and don't exceed them

📋 Follow Traffic Rules

Obey all traffic signals, stop signs, and road regulations

⚠️ Traffic violations can result in fines and legal consequences. Always drive safely and responsibly.

Safety During Household Work

Kitchen Safety

🔪 Sharp Objects

Be careful when using sharp objects or edges like knives, scissors, and broken glass

🔥 Hot Surfaces

Avoid touching hot surfaces and boiling water. Use oven mitts and potholders

🧼 Hand Hygiene

Wash your hands frequently and especially after handling raw meat and other food items

General Household Safety Tips

  • Keep floors dry to prevent slipping
  • Store cleaning products safely away from children
  • Use ladders carefully when reaching high places
  • Unplug electrical appliances when not in use
  • Be careful when handling electrical items near water

What to Do in Emergencies

🚨 999 - Emergency Services

Call for ambulance, fire, or police in urgent situations

📞 995 - LMRA Helpline

For workplace issues, abuse, or questions (24/7, 12 languages)

☎️ 80008008 - Police Hotline

Non-emergency police contact

⚠️ In case of injury:

  • Apply first aid if you can do so safely
  • Inform your employer immediately
  • Seek medical attention at a health centre or hospital
  • Your employer must cover all medical costs
