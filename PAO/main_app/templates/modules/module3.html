What Your Employer Must Give You

If you have signed a tripartite contract, your employer must provide you with:

Your Rights Are Protected

⚠️ Important: If your employer does not provide these things, call 995, even if you do not have a contract.

Your basic rights to accommodation, food, clothing, protective equipment, healthcare, and family contact are protected by Bahraini law, regardless of whether you have a written contract.

Suitable Accommodation

Your accommodation should be:

✓ Safe and Clean

The living space should be hygienic and well-maintained

✓ Private

You should have your own space, ideally a separate room

✓ With Basic Facilities

Access to bathroom, sleeping area, and storage for your belongings

✓ Comfortable

Adequate ventilation, lighting, and temperature control

Your contract should specify whether you will have:

  • Separate room with separate bathroom
  • Separate room with common bathroom
  • Common room with separate bathroom
  • Common room with common bathroom

Food Provisions

Your employer must provide you with adequate food. This means:

🍽️ Regular Meals

Three meals a day or as agreed in your contract

🥗 Nutritious Food

Food that is healthy and sufficient for your needs

🌍 Cultural Considerations

Ideally, food that respects your dietary preferences and cultural background

💡 Tip: If you have dietary restrictions or preferences, discuss this with your employer early on.

Clothing and Protective Equipment

Clothing

Your employer should provide you with appropriate clothing for your work, which may include:

  • Work uniforms (if required)
  • Appropriate footwear for household work
  • Seasonal clothing as needed

Protective Equipment

Your employer must provide protective equipment necessary for your work, such as:

  • Rubber gloves for cleaning and handling chemicals
  • Any other safety equipment needed for specific tasks

⚠️ Safety First: Always use protective equipment when handling cleaning products, sharp objects, or hot surfaces.

Healthcare Provision

Your employer is responsible for your healthcare, including:

🏥 Health Insurance

Your employer must provide health insurance coverage

💊 Medical Treatment

Access to doctors, health centres, and hospitals when needed

💰 Payment for Healthcare

Your employer must pay for your healthcare costs

💡 Remember: If you're sick, tell your employer immediately and ask to see a doctor. Don't rely only on over-the-counter medicine.

Staying Connected with Your Family

Your employer must allow you to have regular contact over the phone with your family. This is an important right that helps you maintain your mental well-being and connection to home.

📱 Phone Access

You should be able to call your family regularly

🌐 Internet Communication

Access to communicate through messaging apps or video calls (if available)

⏰ Private Time

Time and privacy to speak with your loved ones

⚠️ If your employer restricts your communication with family, this is a violation of your rights. Call 995 for help.

If Your Living Conditions Are Not Met

Step 1: Talk to Your Employer

First, try to communicate your concerns directly to your employer

Step 2: Document the Issues

Keep a record of what is not being provided

Step 3: Call for Help

If the situation doesn't improve, call 995 for assistance
