Who is a Domestic Worker?

A domestic worker is anyone who works in a house, whether full time or part time. As a domestic worker, you will make a positive impact on your employer, their family, and Bahraini society.

About Bahrain

Bahrain is divided into several governorates:

  • Capital Governorate - Home to Manama, the capital city
  • Muharraq Governorate - Where Bahrain International Airport is located
  • Northern Governorate
  • Southern Governorate

Important landmarks include:

  • Bahrain World Trade Center
  • Bab Al Bahrain
  • Khamis Mosque
  • Tree of Life
  • Bahrain International Circuit
  • Shaikh Salman bin Ahmed Fort

Weather

Bahrain's weather ranges from around 16 degrees Celsius in the winters to around 48 degrees Celsius in the summers.

💡 Tip: Stay hydrated and try to avoid the sun during peak hours.

Clothing

In Bahrain, you will see people dress in traditional clothing such as thobe and abaya, as well as other kinds of clothing. Bahrain is a diverse country where various styles of dress are common.

Food

Traditional Bahraini food includes:

  • Machboos
  • Balaleet
  • Samboosa
  • Halwa (حلوى بحرينية)
  • Chai Karak (شاي كرك)

You can also find food from your own culture in Bahrain: Indian, Pakistani, Bengali, Filipino, Indonesian, Ethiopian and many more.

Religion

The main religion in Bahrain is Islam. All religions are respected and there are many places of worship around the country.

Ramadan

Muslims observe the holy month of Ramadan once a year, where they fast from sunrise to sunset. During the hours of fasting, eating, drinking and smoking is not permitted in public places.

Languages

Arabic is the main language, but English is widely spoken as well as other languages like Hindi and Urdu.

💡 Tip: Learning your employer's language will help you in your job.

Common greetings:

  • مرحبا (Marhaba) / Ahlan = Hello

Public Transport

You can purchase a GO Card for 500 fils (as of May 2023) to use public buses in Bahrain.

How it works:

  • Add money to the card to pay for your bus trips
  • One trip costs 275 fils
  • Daily limit is 700 fils

Where to purchase:

  • Ticket offices in Manama, Muharraq, and Isa Town
  • Bus terminals
  • Bahrain International Airport

For more information:

  • Visit: www.bahrainbus.bh for weekly bus timetables and costs
  • Call: 66311111

Navigating a New Culture

Tips for Overcoming Culture Shock

📚 Learn the rules of Bahrain

Get to know the culture. Visit www.bahrain.bh for more information on Bahrain.

👥 Make friends

Reach out to your community. Getting to know local people will help you overcome cultural differences and understand the country. It will also show you how to be more sensitive to cultural norms and expectations.

📞 Maintain contact with home

Try to maintain contact with friends and family back home.

🏠 Do something that reminds you of home

This can help you feel connected to your roots while adapting to your new environment.

💪 Exercise and do stretches

Physical activity helps maintain your mental and physical well-being.
