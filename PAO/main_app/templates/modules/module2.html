Your Employment Contract

Tripartite Contract

If you came to Bahrain through an employment office, make sure you have a tripartite contract. If you are recruited through an employment office, you must have a contract. If you don't, call 995.

This is the official contract for domestic workers in Bahrain. It has three parts:

  • The first two parts are signed by your employment office and employer
  • The third part is signed by you and your employer

⚠️ Important: If you didn't come to Bahrain through an employment office or recruiter, you might not have a contract.

💡 Tip: Make sure you understand your contract and keep a copy of it.

What Must My Contract Include?

📋 The nature, type, and duration of the contract

If for a definite period, this should be clearly stated.

👤 The employer's name and address

The workplace address must be included.

🆔 The worker's information

Your name, date of birth, qualifications, job or occupation, residential address, nationality, and necessary personal identification documents.

💰 Wage and benefits

The wage agreed upon, method and time of payment, and all the benefits agreed upon.

Understanding Your Contract Details

Your contract should include information about:

Workplace and Residence

  • Address of workplace (Area, Complex, Street/Road, Building, Apartment)
  • Number of levels, rooms, bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, and bathrooms
  • Whether your residence will be separate from the workplace

Your Accommodation

Your contract should specify if you will have:

  • Separate room with separate bathroom
  • Separate room with common bathroom
  • Common room with separate bathroom
  • Common room with common bathroom

If sharing, it should state how many people share the accommodation.

Employer's Family Information

Your contract should include details about:

  • Total number of family members
  • Whether there are children under 12
  • Whether there are elderly people
  • Whether there are people with special needs or requiring special care
  • Whether there are other domestic workers living with the family

Notice Period Requirements

📅 30 Days Notice

If you want to resign, you must inform your employer at least 30 days in advance in writing.

💵 If you fail to give notice

You must pay your employer compensation equal to one month of your wages.

💵 If your employer fails to give you notice

They must pay you compensation equal to one month of your wages.

Exceptions: When You Don't Need to Give Notice

You do NOT need to give notice if:

1. Assault

Your employer or anyone acting on their behalf assaults you (including verbal assault)

2. Inappropriate Behavior

Inappropriate behavior by your employer or anyone acting on their behalf

⚠️ In these situations, you can resign immediately without giving 30 days notice.
