Emergency Contact: 999

🚨 YOU SHOULD CALL 999 IF YOU ARE IN AN EMERGENCY

When to Call 999:

🚑 When you need an ambulance

Medical emergencies requiring immediate attention

🔥 In case of a fire

Fire emergencies at your workplace or home

👮 When you need to contact the police

Immediate danger, assault, or criminal activity

Police Hotline (Non-Emergency): 80008008

⚠️ 999 is for EMERGENCY situations only. For non-emergency workplace issues, call 995.

Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA): 995

📞 995 - Your Main Support Hotline

In What Situations Can You Call 995?

🆔 Passport and CPR retention

When your employer keeps your documents

🏠 Removal from accommodation

Being forced to leave your living space

⚠️ Abuse and harassment

Any form of mistreatment at work

😟 Threats

Intimidation or threatening behavior

💰 Unpaid wages

Not receiving your salary on time

🚫 Restrictions of movement and communication

Not being allowed to leave or contact family

❓ Any questions you have for LMRA

General inquiries about your employment rights

How to Submit a Complaint About Your Employment

In-Person Complaint Process:

Step 1: Go to the Expat Protection Centre

Location: Sehla

Step 2: Bring Required Documents

  • Letter explaining your complaint
  • Copy of your ID card (CPR)
  • Passport with your residence visa
  • Supporting evidence for your complaint (if available)

Alternative Methods:

📞 Call Expat Protection Centre: 995

👮 Call Police: 80008008

💡 Tip: Keep copies of all documents you submit and note the date and time of your complaint.
