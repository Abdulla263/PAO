Recruitment Fees

⚠️ IMPORTANT: You should NOT pay any costs for your recruitment, visa application, visa renewal, or visa cancellation.

These costs should be covered by:

  • Your employer
  • The employment agency (if applicable)

📞 If you are being asked to pay for any of these services, call 995 immediately.

Services You Should NOT Pay For:

  • Recruitment fees
  • Visa application
  • Visa renewal
  • Visa cancellation
  • Medical examinations required for work permit
  • Work permit fees

Annual Leave

✓ You are entitled to 2.5 days of paid leave per month.

How Annual Leave Works:

After one year of work, you will have accumulated 30 days of paid leave.

Monthly Accumulation:

Month 1: 2.5 days
Month 2: 2.5 days
Month 3: 2.5 days
Month 4: 2.5 days
Month 5: 2.5 days
Month 6: 2.5 days
Month 7: 2.5 days
Month 8: 2.5 days
Month 9: 2.5 days
Month 10: 2.5 days
Month 11: 2.5 days
Month 12: 2.5 days

Total after 12 months: 30 days of paid leave

💡 Tip: Discuss your leave plans with your employer in advance so they can arrange coverage for your duties.

Passport and CPR Retention

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Your employer is NOT allowed to take your passport or CPR (ID card).

Your Rights:

  • You have the right to keep your passport at all times
  • You have the right to keep your CPR (ID card) at all times
  • These documents belong to YOU

What to Do If Your Employer Takes Your Passport or CPR:

Step 1: Ask your employer to return your documents

Step 2: If they refuse, call 995 immediately

📞 LMRA (995) can help you get your passport and CPR back.

Your Right to Live Without Abuse or Harassment

✓ You have the right to live without any abuse or harassment.

Types of Abuse Include:

  • Physical abuse: Hitting, pushing, slapping, or any physical violence
  • Verbal abuse: Shouting, insults, threats, or demeaning language
  • Emotional abuse: Intimidation, isolation, or psychological harm
  • Sexual abuse: Any unwanted sexual contact or harassment
  • Economic abuse: Withholding wages or controlling your money

📞 You can report abuse by calling:

  • 999 - Emergency services (for immediate danger)
  • 995 - LMRA (for workplace issues and support)

💜 Remember: Abuse is NEVER acceptable. You have the right to be treated with dignity and respect.

Unfair Dismissal

Your contract cannot be terminated because of:

❌ Your sex (gender)

❌ Your color (race)

❌ Your religion

❌ Your ideology (beliefs)

❌ Your marital status

❌ Your family responsibilities

❌ Pregnancy

❌ Delivery of a child

❌ Breastfeeding

❌ A complaint you made against your employer

❌ Using your right to take leave

⚠️ If you believe you were dismissed unfairly:

You may be entitled to compensation through a labour court. Call 995 for assistance and guidance on how to file a complaint.

Your Obligations as a Domestic Worker

As a domestic worker, you have certain responsibilities:

✓ Work Well and Perform Your Duties

Complete your assigned tasks to the best of your ability

✓ Respect Your Employer's Privacy

Maintain confidentiality about your employer's personal matters

✓ Take Care of Your Employer's Property

Don't damage your employer's items intentionally

✓ Maintain Professional Conduct

Do not assault your employer or coworkers

✓ Follow Workplace Rules

Do not consume alcohol or drugs at work

⚠️ Consequences of Not Following These Rules:

If you do not follow these rules, your employer can terminate your contract without notice, and you may not receive compensation.

💡 However: You have the right to quit your job any time you want by following the right processes with the LMRA. If you feel uncomfortable in your workplace, contact 995 for help. If you feel in immediate danger, contact 999 and go to the Expat Protection Centre.

Rules About Being Absent from Work

⚠️ ILLEGAL ABSENCES:

It is illegal to be absent from work:

  • For 10 days continuously without a valid reason, OR
  • For 20 days in a year without a valid reason

Consequences of Illegal Absence:

If you leave without following the right processes, you risk:

⚠️ Breaking the law

⚠️ Having your residency permit cancelled

⚠️ Being arrested

⚠️ Being detained

⚠️ Being deported

⚠️ Receiving fines

⚠️ Being banned from re-entering Bahrain

Right to Appeal:

If you are accused of running away, you can appeal within 30 days of being notified. Call 995 for more information on how to do this.

Beware of Scams

⚠️ WARNING:

Be careful of any calls or text messages encouraging you to run away for a higher salary or better working conditions. These are often scammers trying to trick you.

What to Do:

  • Do NOT respond to these messages
  • Inform your employer about these scam attempts
  • Delete suspicious messages
  • Report to 995 if you're concerned

💡 If you feel uncomfortable or unsafe:

  • Contact 995 for workplace issues
  • Contact 999 if you feel in immediate danger

Understanding Irregular Status

What is Irregular Status?

You have an irregular immigration status if:

❌ Using a visit visa for work

It is illegal to use a tourist/visit visa to work in Bahrain

❌ Leaving without proper resignation

If you leave your workplace without resigning and following LMRA's instructions, you could face problems

❌ Staying after visa expires

It is illegal to stay in Bahrain after your visa expires

Risks of Breaking Immigration Rules

The risks of breaking these rules could include:

⚠️ Exploitation

Being taken advantage of or mistreated without legal protection

⚠️ Residency Permit Cancellation

Losing your legal right to stay in Bahrain

⚠️ Arrest and Detention

Being arrested and held by authorities

⚠️ Fines

Having to pay monetary penalties

⚠️ Deportation

Being forced to leave Bahrain

⚠️ Re-entry Ban

Being banned from returning to Bahrain in the future

⚠️ IMPORTANT TO UNDERSTAND:

When people are staying in Bahrain without legal status, it makes it difficult for them to ask for help. It is more likely that you will end up in a situation where you are exploited or treated badly.

Always maintain your legal status and follow proper procedures when changing employment or leaving Bahrain.
